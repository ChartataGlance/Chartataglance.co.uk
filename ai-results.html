How The DAX AI System Works

The system watches DAX chart screenshots, detects trained market structures, saves prediction evidence, and can send Telegram alerts.

1. Capture
Live or historical DAX chart screenshots are captured from the monitor.
2. Detect
The trained YOLO AI model checks for patterns such as 123Buy, BuyWedge, ReverseUp, Bottom, and Double Top.
3. Record
The detected screenshot is saved with pattern name, confidence score, and timestamp.
4. Alert
Telegram can receive the screenshot instantly as a private alert or service notification.
